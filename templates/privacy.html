Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 21, 2026

1. Data We Collect

Spendly collects personal financial information that you voluntarily enter into the application. This includes expense amounts, categories, dates, descriptions, and any notes you add. We also collect account information such as your email address and authentication credentials. No financial account numbers or credit card information is stored on our servers.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your data is used solely to provide the expense tracking functionality of Spendly. We use your information to display your spending history, generate category breakdowns and summaries, and provide filtering by date ranges. Your data is never sold, rented, or shared with third parties for marketing purposes.

3. Data Storage

All your expense data is stored securely in our database. We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information from unauthorized access. Your data is backed up regularly to prevent loss. You retain full ownership of all data you input and can export or delete your data at any time from your account settings.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly uses third-party services for hosting and infrastructure. These providers have access to your data only as necessary to provide their services and are obligated to maintain confidentiality. We do not integrate with external financial institutions or payment processors at this time.

5. Cookies and Analytics

We use essential cookies to maintain your session and provide core functionality. We may collect anonymous usage statistics to improve the application, but this data is not linked to your personal identity or account information.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data at any time. You can export all your expense data or permanently delete your account through the account settings page. If you wish to exercise these rights, please contact us at support@spendly.com.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any material changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date. Continued use of the service after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or our data practices, please contact us at support@spendly.com.
